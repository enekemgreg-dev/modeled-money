## How to fill it in
- **Root query:** the exact phrase typed into YouTube search.
- **Autocomplete suggestions:** everything YouTube suggests as you type the root query, in the order shown.
- **Top 10 results:** the first 10 organic videos (skip ads and Shorts shelves). Record the channel's subscriber count, the video's age, and its views as YouTube displays them.
- **Outlier:** `Y` when views are more than 10 times the channel's subscribers AND the channel has fewer than 10,000 subscribers. Otherwise `N`.
- **Owns "modeled" style:** `Y` when one channel already makes simulation or chart-driven videos on this topic, appearing more than once in the top 10 or with the clear top result. Name the channel in the notes.
- **Searched on:** the date of the search. Results change, so this matters.
